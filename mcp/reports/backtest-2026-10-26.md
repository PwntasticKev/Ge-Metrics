# GE Metrics strategy backtest

Generated 2026-10-26 19:26:58.000Z by `npm run backtest` (mcp/src/backtest). Data: OSRS Wiki /1h and /5m historical snapshots.

## Setup

- 85 decisions every 4h from 2026-10-11 16:00Z to 2026-10-25 16:00Z (UTC); simulation runs to 2026-10-26 18:00Z.
- Stack 50.00M gp, 8 GE slots, one item per slot, cash reserved while a buy offer is open, 4h buy limits, GE tax from the engine (exemptions, 5M cap).
- Fills: hourly. A buy fills only in hours whose average instant-sell price was <= the bid, taking 25% of that hour's instant-sell volume (more if the bid sat above the hour's average, up to all of it at the average high). Sells mirror this on instant-buys at the ask. The sell starts the hour after the buy completes.
- A buy not complete after 8h is cancelled and what was bought goes to the sell. Anything unsold 24h after the buy was placed is dumped at that hour's average instant-sell price.
- No lookahead: at decision time t the strategy sees only windows that closed by t (enforced by a guard that throws; tested). "Latest" prices come from the last closed 5m windows.

## Key findings (analyst notes on this run)

1. **Fixed: series ask lagged falling markets.** `top_flips` / `plan_flips` re-price short-listed items from the median of the last 6 qualifying hourly highs, ignoring the last hour and the live price. In falling markets the ask sat above the market. Pre-fix, picks whose ask was >1% over the last closed hour's average high were 49 filled picks, 55% dumped, **-25.9M realized vs +21.5M expected** (see "pre-fix variants" below). Fix (`refineCandidate`): the series ask is capped at the bulk ask (the last qualifying hour or day, and the live high). After the fix that bucket is gone. `top_flips` gp_per_hour 8h went from **-2.47M (44% max drawdown) to +19.05M (9.8%)**. `plan_flips` 8h / min_volume 50 did not improve (5.76M to 0.36M); its losses come from item 2.
2. **Fixed: plan_flips volume floor was far too low.** Pooled over the fill-aware variants, items trading under 10k a day were 274 filled picks, 33-48% dumped, **-57M realized vs +43M expected**. Items at 10k+ a day realized +187M. Raising `plan_flips`'s default `min_volume` from 50 to 10,000 took `plan_flips` 8h from 0.36M to **24.4M, the best variant, with 10% max drawdown**. `top_flips` also does better at 10k (23.8M vs 19.1M, drawdown 5.2% vs 9.8%). Proposed, not changed: raise `top_flips`'s default from 1,000.
3. **Proposed: sell fill times are too optimistic past ~2h.** Picks predicted to sell in 2-4h were dumped 39% of the time within 24h, and those predicted at 4h+ 56%. Both buckets lost money net. Picks predicted under 2h were dumped 10-13% of the time. Even completed cycles took a median 1.2-1.5x their predicted time (partly the 1h granularity). The fill model treats "hours that reached the price" as independent, but they cluster around spikes. Proposal: demote picks whose sell is predicted above ~2h, or scale predicted hours by ~1.5x. Not shipped: a quick prototype that zeroes sells reached in under 25% / 50% of recent hours was inconsistent across variants (top_flips 8h went 19.0M to 7.6M / 1.9M). This needs out-of-sample data first.
4. **Expected profit is about 2x realized even for the best variants.** On units actually bought, margins were overestimated by 41-53% for the top_flips and plan_flips 10k variants. Trades that sell at the ask realize exactly their predicted margin, so the gap comes from the 15-20% of picks that never sell there and get dumped at the instant-sell price (spread + 2% tax). A reasonable rule of thumb: halve plan_flips' `expectedProfit`.
5. **App Buy now picks lose money in this simulation.** Quick sizes each pick at a 4h slice of daily volume, which assumes the offer gets all of that flow. Only 26% of ordered units were bought and 24% sold at the ask, and 57% of filled picks lost money (-17.2M vs +169M "potential"). Overnight and dips also lost (-5.3M, -11.8M). Proposal for the mobile app: size and price picks with the fill model (as plan_flips does) and show fill-aware GP/h. That is out of scope for this MCP PR.
6. **find_dips and margin_scan barely trade.** find_dips gets 52% of units bought and +0.77M over 14 days. margin_scan fired 3 times and never filled: its guards (fresh live prices, 2x norm, z-score) rarely pass, and the careful bid wasn't reached. Treat both as alerts, not stack strategies.
7. **max_fill_hours:** 4h beat 8h and 12h for `plan_flips` at min_volume 50 (17.0M vs 0.4M vs -1.4M) and for `top_flips` (20.2M vs 19.1M vs 17.8M). Longer patience mostly adds slow sellers (see 3).

**Caveats.** These are in-sample results: fixes 1-2 were chosen on the same 14 days they are scored on. It is one market path, and a handful of large dumps move totals by 10M+, so compare variants by their direction more than exact gp. Fills are hourly. "Live" prices are 5m averages. Buys pay the bid even if the market traded lower. Dumps are not volume-capped. Mapping and buy limits are today's.
